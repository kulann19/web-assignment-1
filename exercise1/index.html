Nikola Tesla

1856 – 1943

About Nikola Tesla

Nikola Tesla was an inventor and electrical engineer born in 1856 in the village of Smiljan, in what is now Croatia. He moved to the United States in 1884 and spent most of his career working on electricity and magnetism. His alternating current system became the standard way of delivering power to homes and factories around the world. Tesla held around three hundred patents and died in New York in 1943.

Important Events

  1. 1856 — born in Smiljan, Austrian Empire
  2. 1884 — arrived in New York and started working for Thomas Edison
  3. 1887 — built the first working alternating current induction motor
  4. 1893 — demonstrated the AC system at the World's Fair in Chicago
  5. 1895 — his design was used for the power plant at Niagara Falls
  6. 1943 — died in New York City at the age of eighty-six

Main Inventions

  • Alternating current induction motor
  • Polyphase system for transmitting electricity
  • The Tesla coil, a high voltage transformer
  • Radio controlled boat, one of the first remote controlled devices
  • Early experiments in wireless transmission of energy
